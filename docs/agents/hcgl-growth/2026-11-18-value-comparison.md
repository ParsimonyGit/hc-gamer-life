## Fact checks (Nov 18, 2026)

1. **53mm.** The live FAQ had no "32mm" (curl, Nov 18). The product page says driver size 53mm. 32 ohms is a different spec. That is probably where "32mm" came from.
2. **Compatibility.** Matches the Trust+Hero table and the live product specs. PC and laptop: yes, with a 3.5mm combo jack. A Y / CTIA splitter is included for separate jacks. PS4: yes. PS5: yes, through the DualSense 3.5mm jack, not the console. Xbox One: yes through a controller with a 3.5mm jack. Controllers from 2015 or earlier need Microsoft's headset adapter, which is not included. Xbox Series X|S: yes, through the controller's 3.5mm jack. Switch handheld: yes. Switch docked: not verified, so do not claim it. Phones: yes with a 3.5mm jack, or a Lightning or USB-C adapter depending on the phone. Say "PC, PlayStation, and Xbox over a 3.5mm jack. Adapters are needed on some devices."
3. **Mic, cable, isolation.** Product data: detachable microphone, yes. Mute and volume are on the inline controller. Cable: permanently attached, about 3 ft, with the removable PC splitter extending the run to about 6 ft. Single 3.5mm CTIA TRRS plug. Closed-back. No noise-cancelling claim in the product data.
4. **Warranty.** Sold without warranty. The older multi-year warranty lines are withdrawn. Do not put them back into ads, briefs, or the theme.
5. **Returns.** 30-day returns. The buyer pays return shipping unless the unit is defective, damaged, or wrong. Defective units replaced within 90 days.
6. **Price, end, shipping.** BF60 about $52. BF70 about $39 each on 5 or more. Through Nov 30, 2026, 11:59 PM CT. Free continental US shipping. Ships from Idaho in 1–2 business days. No Christmas cutoff date.
7. **Closing language.** None in the gift block except the sale-terms line "Final stock. We are not restocking."
